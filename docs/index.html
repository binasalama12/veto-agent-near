🛡️ VetoAgent — VetoGuard dashboard

An AI agent's money, guarded by a NEAR smart contract. Every payment the agent attempts is checked on-chain against its owner's policy — per-transaction cap, daily cap, recipient allowlist, and an owner kill-switch. Approved payments execute; everything else is vetoed and written to a public audit log. Live on NEAR testnet.
